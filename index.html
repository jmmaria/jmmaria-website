About

Hi — I'm JM Maria.

I'm a travel and lifestyle content creator based in Dubai. I make the kind of films that make you want to go somewhere: a hotel room at first light, the café everyone in Jumeirah is talking about, a ferry pulling into Phi Phi.

It started as filming my own trips. It turned into work — ten properties across five countries, nine Dubai venues, and an audience who plan their trips around what I post. Brands started asking for the same thing for their own channels, so now I shoot for them too.

I work alone, and that's the point. I scout, film, edit and deliver everything myself. What you approve is what you get, and the tone holds from the first frame to the last cut.

About

Hi — I'm JM Maria.

I'm a travel and lifestyle content creator based in Dubai. I make the kind of films that make you want to go somewhere: a hotel room at first light, the café everyone in Jumeirah is talking about, a ferry pulling into Phi Phi.

It started as filming my own trips. It turned into work — ten properties across five countries, nine Dubai venues, and an audience who plan their trips around what I post. Brands started asking for the same thing for their own channels, so now I shoot for them too.

I work alone, and that's the point. I scout, film, edit and deliver everything myself. What you approve is what you get, and the tone holds from the first frame to the last cut.

How it works

Four steps, from brief to published.

01

Brief

You send the property or venue, the dates and what the content needs to do. I come back with a scope, deliverables list and a quote — usually within 24 hours.

02

Scout

Shot list built around your spaces and your peak light. If there are hero rooms, signature dishes or angles you need covered, they go on the list before I arrive.

03

Shoot

Filmed on location, typically in a single visit for venues and one to two days for properties. Minimal crew and no disruption to guests or service.

04

Deliver

Master edit plus vertical cuts, delivered to your drive with one revision round included. Published on my channels too, if that's part of the agreement.

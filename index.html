About

Hi — I'm JM Maria.

I'm a travel and lifestyle creator based in Dubai. I make the kind of films that make you want to go somewhere: a hotel room at first light, the café everyone in Jumeirah is talking about, a ferry pulling into Phi Phi.

Ten properties across five countries, nine Dubai venues, and an audience who plan their trips around what I post. I scout, film, edit and publish all of it myself.
